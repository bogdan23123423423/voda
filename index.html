The origin

A source, not a factory.

Most water travels through pipelines, blending dozens of sources into one uniform taste. AQUU comes from a single spring, high in ancient basalt rock, and never touches another source.

What leaves the ground is what arrives at your table — nothing added, nothing removed, nothing rushed.

The same shape, every time, so the only thing that changes bottle to bottle is how many times you've brought it back.
